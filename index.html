MODEL & EVIDENCE

A plant you can inspect.

OpenPWR couples the major energy pathways of a generic 1,000 MWe PWR. The source, equations, assumptions, and verification are open to inspection.

01 / REACTOR

Neutron kinetics & feedback

Six delayed-neutron groups, fuel and moderator feedback, iodine/xenon dynamics, and time-dependent decay heat.

02 / THERMAL SYSTEMS

Connected heat & mass balances

Fuel, primary coolant, steam generation, secondary inventory, feedwater, and condenser dynamics exchange heat and mass.

03 / CONTROL

Startup & operating controls

A cold-start sequence exposes state-based permissives. Heat-up, turbine roll, and grid connection follow the model; demand and disturbances then propagate through the connected systems.

04 / REPRODUCIBILITY

Deterministic experiments

Fixed simulation steps, complete state serialization, long-duration trends, and published component benchmarks with source-by-source reference comparisons.

Where this model stops

This is a generic, reduced-order system simulation. It does not resolve 3D neutron transport, detailed two-phase flow, fuel damage, containment, or severe accidents. Parameters and trip limits are generic assumptions. Automated verification is not plant-specific validation or operator-training qualification.

When a numerical or physical validity boundary is exceeded, the experiment pauses and reports the reason.
