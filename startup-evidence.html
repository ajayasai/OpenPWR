      What the meters can tell us

      The station record shows when electricity returned and how output changed between half-hour readings. The native run starts at a prescribed cold state and follows the simulator’s own control schedule. The two charts use independent clocks and power scales. The meter readings alone cannot establish reactor temperature, criticality, breaker timing or pressure accuracy.
